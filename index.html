Dispute Resolution & Additional Terms

Binding Arbitration. Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Waiver, or my participation in Southbay Hoops activities, shall be resolved exclusively by binding arbitration administered in Santa Clara County, California, rather than in court, except that either party may bring an individual claim in small claims court if it qualifies.

Entire Agreement. This Waiver is the entire agreement between me and the Released Parties regarding these matters and supersedes any prior oral or written understandings. I have had the opportunity to seek independent legal advice before signing.
